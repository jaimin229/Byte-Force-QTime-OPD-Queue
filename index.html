🧠 Bayesian Waiting Time Prediction Engine

Rather than static averages or naive linear extrapolation, QTime utilizes an empirical Bayes normal-normal conjugate model:

μ_post = (κ₀ · μ₀ + n · x̄) / (κ₀ + n)
• Global Prior μ₀ = 420s (7.0 min standard OPD)
• Prior Weight κ₀ = 5 pseudo-observations
• Dynamically calibrated against crowd taps & doctor consultation finishes.

🏥 About QTime — The Live OPD Queue Platform

Built for Mini-Hackathon 2026 · Squad: Byte Force
🎯 The Problem

Patients in Indian district hospitals wait 2 to 4 hours in crowded, unhygienic OPD corridors without knowing when they will be seen. Static token boards are misleading because doctor consultation times vary dramatically.

💡 The Solution ("Waze for OPDs")

Live queue sync powered by Bayesian adaptive ETA shrinkage, two-way crowd calibration ("I'm Being Seen"), 5-away stepping out alerts, lab test rejoin priority, and cinema TV waiting displays.
