Empirical Bayes Waiting Time Prediction Formula

Rather than static linear extrapolations or naive queue arithmetic, QTime utilizes an empirical Bayes normal-normal conjugate model:

μ_post = (κ₀ · μ₀ + n · x̄) / (κ₀ + n)

• Prior Mean μ₀ = 420s (7.0 min clinic benchmark)
• Prior Weight κ₀ = 5 pseudo-observations
• n = Completed consultations today
• x̄ = Observed sample mean duration
• Calibrated in real time against doctor serving timestamps and patient crowd taps.

About QTime — Live OPD Queue & Bayesian Waiting Time Predictor

Engineered for Mini-Hackathon 2026 · Squad: Byte Force · Author: Jaimin Prajapati (@jaimin229)
The Problem

Patients in Indian district hospitals wait 2 to 4 hours in crowded, unhygienic OPD corridors with zero visibility into doctor pace, emergency breaks, or diagnostic delays. Static token boards are misleading because consultation durations have high variance.

The Solution ("Waze for OPDs")

Live queue synchronization powered by Bayesian adaptive ETA shrinkage, bidirectional crowd calibration ("I'm Being Seen Now"), 5-away stepping out proximity alerts, lab test return priority locks, and cinema TV waiting displays.
